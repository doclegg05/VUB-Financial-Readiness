2. The Family Binder

One-page account list with institution names and phone numbers.
Copies of beneficiary confirmations, not just old forms.
Names for attorney, tax preparer, financial advisor, VSO, and funeral home.
Instructions for where passwords are stored, without printing passwords on this handout.

3. Review Triggers

Marriage, divorce, remarriage, birth, adoption, or death in the family.
Move to a new state or major change in state law.
New diagnosis, cognitive concern, or major care need.
At least once each year, even when nothing changed.

Facilitator guidance

No personal information should be collected in class. A complete student answer should identify which forms need verification, where the student will store proof, and which person or professional should know where the documents are located.
