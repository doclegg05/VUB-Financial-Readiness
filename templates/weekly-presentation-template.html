Two Column Contrast

Ideal for comparisons, pros/cons, or parallel lists

✔️ Best Practices

  • Short bullet points
  • Clear action verbs
  • High contrast icons

⚠️ Things to Avoid

  • Long paragraphs
  • Tiny fonts (<24pt on display)
  • Overwhelming graphics
⚠️ Remember
Ensure you do not rely on color alone to convey difference. Add symbols or labels.
