# Week 3 — "Creating & Communicating" Lesson Video — Narration Script

Follows the approved Week 2 reference (`week2-video-script.md`) exactly in shape, depth, and warm tone.
Audience: older U.S. military veterans, newer to computers. Format: **watched straight through; the
hands-on lab happens after.** Target 6–8 min, ~120–135 wpm, every term defined on first use. Maps to the
existing lab `courses/digital-literacy-1/weeks/week-03/handouts/make-save-share-lab.html`
(Ch.1 → Lab Parts 1 & 2 · Ch.2 → Lab Part 3 · Ch.3 → Lab Parts 4 & 5).

Scene-kinds: `chapterCard` · `uiCallout` · `compare` · `steps` · `question`.

screenshots** (Scene 9 the Word "Save As" dialog with a clear file name, Scene 13 the Ctrl+P print
screen with the orientation control and page preview). Everything else is built in code.

`w3l-NN.mp3` clip. For the three `question` scenes the spoken line is the full "question … answer …
why," and the answers are reused verbatim from the lesson's Knowledge Check (slide 16).
