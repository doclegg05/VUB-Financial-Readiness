# Week 5 — "Review & Testing Day" Lesson Video — Narration Script

Tailored REVIEW edition (Weeks 1, 3, 4 follow the Week 2 master template; Week 5 is the review).
Audience: older U.S. military veterans, newer to computers. Format: **watched straight through;
the hands-on review and the Post-Test happen after.** Target 5–6 min, ~125 wpm, every term
defined on first use. Warmly recaps the five topic areas from Weeks 1–4, framed like a friendly
in-class review game, then a calm Post-Test lead-in, then a look ahead to Level 2.

Maps to `courses/digital-literacy-1/weeks/week-05/presentation.html` (the Jeopardy-style review
game + Post-Test) and the take-home `handouts/level-1-study-guide.html`. The four review areas fold
the presentation's seven IC3 GS6 categories into the way the course was taught week by week:
Area 1 = Hardware & Operating Systems (Wk 1) · Area 2 = Getting Online (Wk 2) ·
Area 3 = Creating & Communicating (Wk 3) · Area 4 = Citizenship & Safety (Wk 4).

Scene-kinds: `chapterCard` · `uiCallout` · `compare` · `steps` · `question`.
Assets the user supplies for this video: **2 photoreal hero images** (Scenes 1 & 23). Everything
else is built in code. The six `question` scenes use the EXACT review-game questions and reveal
answers from the presentation.
