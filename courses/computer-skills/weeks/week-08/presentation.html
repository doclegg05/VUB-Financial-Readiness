Why Go Mobile?

Your skills work everywhere

The Power of Mobile
Everything you learned on the computer works on your phone too! Check email at the doctor's office, access files while traveling, stay connected anywhere.

📱 Benefits

  • Access anywhere, anytime
  • Get notifications instantly
  • Share photos easily
  • Stay connected with family

🔗 Same Accounts

  • Your Gmail works on phone
  • Google Drive syncs everywhere
  • One login, all devices
  • Changes appear everywhere

Gmail on Your Phone

Email in your pocket

📧

Gmail App

Free download from App Store (iPhone) or Play Store (Android)

  1. Download the Gmail app
    Search "Gmail" in your phone's app store
  2. Sign in with your Google account
    Use the same email and password from class
  3. Allow notifications
    Get alerts when new emails arrive
Everything Syncs!
Emails you read on your phone will show as read on your computer too. Your labels and contacts are all there.

Google Drive Mobile

Your files, everywhere

📁

Google Drive App

Access all your cloud files from your phone

📱 What You Can Do

  • View all your folders
  • Open documents and photos
  • Upload photos from your phone
  • Share files with family

📸 Photo Backup Tip

  • Turn on "Backup & Sync"
  • Photos auto-save to Drive
  • Never lose memories!
  • Access photos on computer
