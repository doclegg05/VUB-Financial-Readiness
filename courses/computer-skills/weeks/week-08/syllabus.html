Instructor Notes

Key Teaching Points

  • Mobile Setup: Not everyone will have a smartphone. Focus on demonstrating concepts. Those with phones can follow along; others can set up at home using the handout.
  • Review Pacing: Keep the review high-level. Hit key points rather than re-teaching. Use it to refresh memory before the post-test.
  • Post-Test Environment: Create a calm, supportive atmosphere. Remind students this isn't pass/fail—it measures growth.
  • Comparison Feature: The post-test will automatically compare to pre-test results if available in localStorage. This creates a powerful "before/after" moment.
  • Celebration: This is a significant achievement! Many adults are intimidated by technology. Completing this course is worthy of genuine celebration.

WIPPEA Pacing & Workshop Logistics

  • Distribute Review Workshop handouts at 0:25 (start of Practice phase)
  • Part 1 is GUIDED — walk through mobile setup step by step
  • Part 2 is INTERACTIVE — Skill Bingo gets students up and moving
  • Allow flexibility: some students won't have smartphones — they write down steps for home
  • Bingo game serves as both review AND energy boost before the post-test
  • Keep post-test atmosphere calm and encouraging — "This measures growth, not grades"

🎉 Celebration Moment!

Take time to genuinely celebrate your students' achievements. 8 weeks of learning technology is no small feat. Consider sharing improvement statistics and personal growth stories.

Post-Test Administration

Post-Test Checklist

  • Ensure post-test.html is loaded on all student computers
  • Verify printer is ready for PDF reports
  • Remind students: 20 questions, no time limit, can change answers
  • Encourage students to take their time and read carefully
  • Assist with printing PDF reports after completion
  • If pre-test data exists, students will see their improvement!

Review Topics Checklist

Quick Review Points by Week

  • Week 1: VA.gov navigation, MyHealtheVet messaging, ID.me verification, eBenefits
  • Week 2: Keyboard shortcuts (Ctrl+C/V/Z), window snapping, screenshots, accessibility settings
  • Week 3: Gmail compose, CC/BCC, attachments, labels, signatures, search operators
  • Week 4: Word styles, headers/footers, tables, page numbers, spell check
  • Week 5: Excel formulas (SUM, AVERAGE, MAX, MIN), formatting, charts, sorting/filtering
  • Week 6: Google Drive folders, uploading, sharing, Google Docs, access from anywhere
  • Week 7: ChatGPT conversations, prompt engineering, real-world tasks, fact-checking, hallucination awareness
