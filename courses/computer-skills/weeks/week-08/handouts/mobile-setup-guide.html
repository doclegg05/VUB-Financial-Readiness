Why Use Mobile Apps?

Your Gmail and Google Drive work on your phone too! This means you can check email, view files, and even upload photos while away from your computer. Everything stays in sync automatically.

Setting Up Gmail on Your Phone

📧

Gmail App

Free email app from Google

🤖 Android Phone

  1. Open the Play Store app
  2. Search for "Gmail"
  3. Tap "Install" (may already be installed)
  4. Open Gmail app
  5. Tap "Add account"
  6. Enter your Gmail address
  7. Enter your password
  8. Allow notifications when asked

🍎 iPhone

  1. Open the App Store
  2. Search for "Gmail"
  3. Tap "Get" to download
  4. Open Gmail app
  5. Tap "Sign in"
  6. Enter your Gmail address
  7. Enter your password
  8. Allow notifications when asked
💡 Tip: Use the same Gmail address you created in Week 3. All your emails, contacts, and labels will be there!

Setting Up Google Drive on Your Phone

📁

Google Drive App

Access all your cloud files on the go

🤖 Android Phone

  1. Open the Play Store app
  2. Search for "Google Drive"
  3. Tap "Install" (may already be installed)
  4. Open Drive app
  5. Sign in with your Google account
  6. You'll see all your folders!

🍎 iPhone

  1. Open the App Store
  2. Search for "Google Drive"
  3. Tap "Get" to download
  4. Open Drive app
  5. Sign in with your Google account
  6. You'll see all your folders!
💡 Tip: Tap the camera icon in Drive to scan documents or upload photos directly to the cloud!

How Syncing Works

🔄 Automatic Syncing

  • Changes you make on your phone appear on your computer
  • Changes you make on your computer appear on your phone
  • Emails you read on your phone show as read everywhere
  • Files you upload from your phone appear in Drive on your computer
  • Everything stays the same across all your devices!

Helpful Features

  • Notifications: Get alerts when new emails arrive
  • Search: Find emails and files quickly using the search bar
  • Offline Access: Mark files to be available without internet
  • Share: Send files directly from your phone
  • Photo Backup: Automatically save phone photos to Drive

Mobile Safety Tips

🔒 Keep Your Phone Secure

  • Use a screen lock (PIN, pattern, fingerprint, or face)
  • Don't save passwords in browsers on shared devices
  • Be careful using public WiFi for sensitive tasks
  • Log out of apps if others use your phone
  • Keep your apps updated for security fixes
  • Don't click links in suspicious text messages

Quick Reference

Task App to Use
Check email Gmail
Send email with attachment Gmail (tap paperclip icon)
View your files Google Drive
Upload a photo Google Drive (tap + button)
Share a file Google Drive (tap ⋮ then Share)
Scan a document Google Drive (tap + then Scan)

Troubleshooting

App won't sign in? Make sure you're using the correct email address and password. Try the "Forgot password" option if needed.
Not seeing recent files? Pull down on the screen to refresh, or check your internet connection.
Running out of space? Go to Settings in the app to manage storage or delete old items you don't need.
