Week 4: Microsoft Word Intermediate

Instructor Guide
📅 Week 4 of 8 ⏱️ 2 Hours (WIPPEA) 👥 Veterans Group 📍 Computer Lab

🎯 Learning Objectives

By the end of this session, participants will be able to:

  • Apply built-in styles for consistent document formatting
  • Find and customize templates for common documents
  • Create and format tables for organized data
  • Add headers, footers, and page numbers
  • Use spell check and review tools
  • Follow formatting best practices for professional documents
  • Apply formatting skills independently through guided workshop
  • Create a complete VA Appeal Letter with styles, tables, and headers
⏰ WIPPEA Session Timeline (120 Minutes)
0:00-0:08

Document Makeover Warm-Up

Show bad vs professional document side-by-side. Discussion: "Which would you trust?" Surface what makes documents look professional (headings, consistent fonts, tables). Connect to Week 3 email skills.

0:08-0:15

Today's Roadmap Introduction

Preview the 2-hour plan. Distribute Word Document Workshop handout. What they'll build: VA Appeal Letter with styles, tables, headers.

0:15-0:35

Core Presentation Presentation

Move briskly through slides 3-14. Key topics: Word interface/ribbon, styles (Title, Heading 1, Heading 2), templates, creating & formatting tables, headers & footers, page numbers, formatting best practices, spell check. Keep concise since students will practice hands-on.

0:35-0:55

Workshop Parts 1 & 2 Guided Practice

Part 1 - Styles Foundation (15 min): Walk through creating VA Appeal Letter with Title and Heading 1 styles, using Navigation Pane. Part 2 - Tables for Data (15 min): Build service history table (3 cols x 4 rows) and medical evidence table (2 cols x 3 rows), apply table styles.

0:55-1:05

Break Break

Save work, stretch. Check students' progress on Parts 1-2.

1:05-1:45

Workshop Parts 3 & 4 Independent Practice

Part 3 - Headers, Footers & Page Numbers (10 min): Students add header with name/date, centered page numbers, page break with new section. Part 4 - Polish & Review (20 min): Spell check, bullet lists, bold key terms, margins, print preview, save document. Early finishers try bonus challenges.

1:45-1:55

Show & Share Evaluation

Partner check: show VA Appeal Letter. Peer review checklist: styles applied? Tables formatted? Header/footer present? Spell check done? Volunteers share. Knowledge Check questions.

1:55-2:00

Word Challenge & Preview Application

At-home challenge: create medication tracking doc, write a formal letter with styles, email the VA Appeal Letter to yourself. Preview Week 5: Excel Intermediate.

📝 Instructor Notes

  • Pacing: Lecture is only 20 minutes. Move briskly - students practice each skill in the workshop.
  • Workshop Handout: Distribute during Introduction phase.
  • Guided vs Independent: Parts 1 & 2 are instructor-led. Parts 3 & 4 are student-driven.
  • Word Versions: Most concepts work in Word 2016, 2019, 2021, and 365. Interface may vary slightly - point out any differences you notice.
  • Styles Concept: This is often new for participants. Use the analogy of "presets" in a camera - one-click formatting.
  • Table Navigation: Emphasize Tab key to move between cells. Many try to click each cell individually.
  • Save Often: Remind them to save frequently. Consider demonstrating AutoSave for 365 users.
  • Practical Examples: Use veteran-relevant examples: VA appeal letter, medication list, contact sheet.
  • Don't Overwhelm: Focus on the basics. Advanced features like mail merge can wait for another course.

💬 Discussion Prompts by WIPPEA Phase

  • Warm-Up (Document Makeover): "Which of these two documents would you trust more - the messy one or the professional one? What makes the difference?"
  • Introduction (Today's Roadmap): "What kinds of documents do you create most often? Letters? Lists? Have you ever struggled to make them look professional?"
  • Presentation (Core Slides): "Can you see how using styles would be faster than manually changing fonts and sizes each time? What documents could you use this for?"
  • Guided Practice (Workshop Parts 1-2): "What information in your life could be organized in a table? Medications, appointments, contacts, expenses?"
  • Evaluation (Show & Share): "What was the most useful skill you practiced today? What would you do differently next time?"
  • Application (Word Challenge): "What's one document you'll create at home this week using what you learned today?"

✅ Tips for Success

  • Walk around constantly - many need individual help finding ribbon items
  • Show each step slowly on projector, then have them do it, then move on
  • If someone falls behind, help them catch up before the next topic
  • Encourage peer help - early finishers can assist neighbors
  • Remind them F7 for spell check - emphasize checking before sharing documents
  • Connect to previous weeks: "Now you can create a document and email it!"
