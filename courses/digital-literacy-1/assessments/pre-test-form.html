Instructor setup required

This page is ready to embed a Google Form for graded Pre-Test capture, but no form URL has been added yet. Students can use the offline self-test at pre-test.html in the meantime.

  1. Create the Pre-Test as a Google Form (questions are in pre-test-printable.html).
  2. In the Form, choose Send → < > (embed) and copy the src URL.
  3. Paste that URL into the embed iframe in this file's source (replace its srcdoc attribute with a src attribute set to that URL).
