🔒 Password & Passphrase Rules (start here!)

Make it longUse a passphrase of 12+ characters — e.g. PurpleTrout-Coffee-Sunrise7
Use unrelated wordsRandom words are strong and easy to remember; add a capital and a number
One per accountNever reuse the same password on important accounts
Don't make it guessableNo names, birthdays, "password," or "123456"
Extra protectionTurn on two-factor authentication; consider a password manager
Keep it secretNever share it, and don't write it on a sticky note on your monitor

Lock & Reset

Win + LLock your screen instantly
Step away?Always lock it first
Forgot password?Click "Forgot password?" on the sign-in page
Verify identityConfirm by email or text code, then set a new passphrase
Account hacked?Reset the password right away

Clear Browser Data

Ctrl+Shift+DelOpen "Clear browsing data"
ChooseHistory, cookies, cached files
Time range"Last hour" or "All time"
NoteClearing cookies signs you out of sites

Private Browsing

Ctrl+Shift+NIncognito (Chrome)
Ctrl+Shift+PInPrivate (Edge / Firefox)
It doesSkip saving history & cookies on this device
It does NOTHide you from your provider, network, or sites you log into

👁 Spot Fake Info & Scams

  • Who wrote it? Look for a named author or organization
  • Where? Trust .gov, major news, and known organizations
  • When? Check for a recent date
  • Confirm it on a second trusted source
  • No author or source listed
  • Shocking headline or urgent "act now" tone
  • Spelling and grammar errors
  • Asks for money or personal information
Trolls? Don't reply. Block, report, and walk away.

💼 Desk Ergonomics

  • Screen about an arm's length away
  • Top of screen at or just below eye level
  • Sit back, lower back supported, feet flat
  • Wrists straight, elbows near 90°
  • 20-20-20: every 20 min, look 20 ft away for 20 sec
  • Stand and stretch every half hour

Protect Your PII

  • Never send SSN or passwords by email/text
  • Look for the lock icon (https) before you type it
  • The VA and banks never ask for passwords by email
One habit a day: a long passphrase, Win+L when you leave, and clearing data on shared PCs keep you safe.
