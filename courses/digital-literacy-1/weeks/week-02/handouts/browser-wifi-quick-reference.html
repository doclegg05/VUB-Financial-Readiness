🔧 Wi-Fi Troubleshooting (try in order, stop when it works)
  1. Check the network icon — Wi-Fi on? Right network? (Globe = not connected.)
  2. Try another website — if only one fails, that site is the problem.
  3. Restart the modem/router — unplug, wait 30 seconds, plug back in, wait 2 minutes. (Fixes most problems.)
  4. Restart your computer — clears small glitches.
  5. Call your internet provider — there may be an outage in your area.
🔍 Smart Search Tips
  1. Start with your need — decide your question before you type.
  2. Use a few clear words — 3–4 specific words beat a full sentence.
  3. Skip the ads — results marked "Ad" or "Sponsored" come first; look past them.
  4. Trust good sources — prefer .gov / .edu and well-known organizations.
  5. Keep your source — bookmark it or copy the web address, and note the date.
  6. Respect copyright — most content belongs to someone; look for public domain or Creative Commons, or give credit.
Pro tip: Ctrl+F finds any word on a page or in a document — the single most useful shortcut for getting answers fast!
