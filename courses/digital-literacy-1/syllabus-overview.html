Course Description

This 5-week course builds foundational digital literacy for veterans, mapped to the official IC3 GS6 Level 1 objective domains. Across four lesson weeks and a review & testing day, students gain hands-on experience with computer hardware and operating systems, getting online and searching, creating and sharing documents, communicating and collaborating, and protecting their identity, devices, and wellbeing online. It is the first rung of a planned Level 1 → Level 2 → Level 3 ladder.

Learning Outcomes (IC3 GS6 Level 1)

  • Identify computer devices, ports, memory vs. storage, software types, and operating systems (Domain 1).
  • Navigate browsers and digital environments; understand networks and Wi-Fi and basic troubleshooting (Domain 1).
  • Search effectively, find information with Ctrl+F, and recognize copyright, public domain, and Creative Commons (Domain 3).
  • Create, name, save, back up, and print basic documents; cite sources appropriately (Domain 4).
  • Communicate and collaborate online — email response options, inclusive language, etiquette, and synchronous vs. asynchronous work (Domains 5 & 6).
  • Manage a digital identity and reputation; evaluate online information (Domain 2).
  • Protect devices and content with strong passwords, locking, and private browsing; recognize tracking and health risks (Domain 7).

Course Schedule

Week Lesson Key Activities & IC3 Domains
Week 1 Inside the Computer Pre-Test; input/output devices & ports, hardware (memory vs. storage), software (paid vs. free, installing safely), operating systems. Lab: tour the lab PC. (Domain 1: 1.2–1.5)
Week 2 Getting Online Browsers & digital environments, networks/Wi-Fi & troubleshooting, smart searching, Ctrl+F, copyright/Creative Commons. Lab: search & connect. (Domains 1.1, 1.6, 3)
Week 3 Creating & Communicating Create/save/name/back up documents, citations, printing; email response options, inclusive language; collaboration & etiquette. Lab: make, save & share. (Domains 4, 5, 6)
Week 4 Citizenship & Safety Digital identity, PII & reputation, evaluating online info; strong passwords, locking, clearing data, private browsing, tracking; mental & physical health. Lab: lock it down. (Domains 2, 7)
Week 5 Review & Testing Day Cumulative review game across all seven topic areas, then the Post-Test; wrap-up and a look ahead to Level 2. (All domains)

Assessment & Materials

A Pre-Test (Week 1) and Post-Test (Week 5) measure growth using a 20-question exam written to the IC3 GS6 Level 1 objectives. Flashcards and a practice quiz are available any time as study resources. Weekly hands-on labs and printable quick-reference cards reinforce each lesson. Materials: a lab computer (Windows), the provided handouts, and a USB flash drive are helpful but optional.
