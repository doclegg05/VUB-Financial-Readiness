What is ChatGPT?

ChatGPT is an AI assistant that can help you write, answer questions, explain topics, and assist with planning. It works like having a conversation with a very knowledgeable helper. It's free to use!

Writing Good Prompts

A "prompt" is your message to the AI. Better prompts get better answers!

The Prompt Formula:

Strong Prompt = WHAT you want + WHO it's for + HOW you want it

  • WHAT — Say exactly what you need (a letter, an explanation, a list)
  • WHO — Give context about the audience (for a veteran, for a beginner)
  • HOW — Specify the tone, length, or format (formal, under 200 words, bullet points)

Compare These Examples:

Weak Prompt:
"Write a letter"
Strong Prompt:
"Help me write a brief, formal letter to the VA requesting a copy of my service records. Keep it professional and to the point."
Weak Prompt:
"What's Medicare?"
Strong Prompt:
"Explain the difference between Medicare Part A and Part B in simple terms that a beginner can understand."

Do's and Don'ts

Do:

  • Ask for help with writing
  • Request explanations of topics
  • Use it for planning and lists
  • Ask follow-up questions
  • Say "make it simpler" or "make it shorter"
  • Verify important information elsewhere

Don't:

  • Share personal information
  • Trust it for medical advice
  • Believe everything without checking
  • Use it for legal decisions
  • Share passwords or account numbers
  • Expect it to know current news

Stay Safe!

NEVER Share These With AI:

  • Social Security Number
  • Bank account or credit card numbers
  • Passwords or PINs
  • Medical record numbers
  • Your full address
  • Date of birth

Remember:

AI is a helpful tool, but it's not perfect. Always double-check important information with official sources (like VA.gov for benefits questions).
