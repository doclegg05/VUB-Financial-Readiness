Week 6: Cloud Computing & Google Drive

VUB Intermediate Computer Skills

Instructor Guide
📅 March 16, 2026 ⏱️ 2 Hours (WIPPEA) 👥 Veterans Group 📍 Computer Lab

🎯 Learning Objectives

  • Explain what "the cloud" means in simple terms
  • Navigate the Google Drive interface
  • Upload files from computer to Google Drive
  • Create and organize folders in Drive
  • Share files with specific people or via link
  • Create a basic Google Doc
  • Collaborate on a shared Google Doc with real-time editing
  • Complete a multi-part hands-on workshop independently
⏰ WIPPEA Session Timeline (120 Minutes)
0:00-0:08

Warm-Up Warm-Up

Coffee Shop Disaster scenario: you're at the VA office, need your DD-214, but your computer is at home. Discussion: when have you been stuck without a file you needed?

0:08-0:15

Introduction Intro

What is the cloud? Today's agenda and preview of what they'll build: an organized Drive with 4 folders, a shared doc, and access from any device. Connect to Week 5 budget — we'll upload it to Drive.

Core lecture: cloud concept (safe deposit box analogy), Google Drive interface, uploading methods, folder creation, sharing permissions (Viewer/Editor), Google Docs intro + auto-save.

Cloud & Google Drive Workshop Parts 1-2: Create 4 color-coded folders, upload 3 files, rename, star, organize into folders. Distribute workshop handout at 0:35.

0:55-1:05

Files auto-saved in cloud — no need to save! Stretch, questions.

1:05-1:45

Workshop Parts 3-4: Create shared Google Doc "Class Contact List" with a classmate (real-time collaboration). Access Anywhere Challenge: incognito window verification, optional phone check.

Cross-device verification: show neighbor your folders, verify shared doc appears for both, check Drive in incognito window. Phone bonus check.

📝 Instructor Notes

  • Workshop Handout: Distribute the Cloud & Google Drive Workshop handout at the 0:35 mark when guided practice begins.
  • Guided vs. Independent: Parts 1-2 are instructor-led (guided). Walk through each step on the projector. Parts 3-4 are student-driven (independent) — circulate and assist.
  • Pacing: If Gmail login issues eat into time, shorten the presentation and go straight to guided practice. The workshop handout covers all key concepts.
  • Gmail Login: They'll need their Gmail from Week 3. Have password reset ready as backup.
  • Security Concerns: Some may be hesitant about "putting files online." Reassure them about Google's security, but also remind them not to store extremely sensitive items (SSN, banking passwords).
  • The Cloud Analogy: "Bank safe deposit box" works well. Also: "Like having a copy of your house key at a trusted friend's house."
  • Auto-Save Magic: The auto-save feature really impresses people. Point it out explicitly — "Notice you don't have to click Save!"
  • Phone Access: If time permits, show how they can access Drive from their phones — this often clicks as a huge benefit.

💬 Discussion Questions

  • Warm-Up: "Has anyone ever needed a file that was at home when you weren't? What happened?"
  • After Presentation: "Does the 'bank safe deposit box' analogy help? What files would you want in your cloud safe deposit box?"
  • Guided Practice: "How does it feel knowing your files are now backed up in two places? Which folder will get the most files?"
  • Evaluation: "Were you able to access your files from the incognito window? What surprised you about real-time collaboration?"
  • Wrap-Up: "What's the first important document you'll upload to Drive this week?"

✅ Tips for Success

  • Gmail login issues are common — budget extra time at the start if needed
  • The drag-and-drop upload method is intuitive but requires two windows side by side
  • When demonstrating sharing, use your instructor email so they can practice sharing with you
  • Emphasize that Google Docs is FREE — this surprises many people
  • Connect to previous weeks: "You can upload those Word docs or Excel files we made!"
  • If someone finishes early, have them try accessing Drive from their phone
