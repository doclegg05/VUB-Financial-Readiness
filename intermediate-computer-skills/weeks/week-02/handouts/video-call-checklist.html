✅ Video Call Checklist

VUB Intermediate Computer Skills - Week 2

HANDOUT
Use this checklist before every important video call! Being prepared helps ensure a smooth experience and makes a good impression.

🔧 Before Your Call (15 Minutes Ahead)

  • Test your camera

    Go to Settings > Privacy > Camera to make sure it's working

  • Test your microphone

    Go to Settings > Privacy > Microphone to verify it's enabled

  • Check your speakers or headphones

    Make sure volume is up and you can hear audio

  • Find the meeting link or ID

    Check your email or text for the meeting information

  • Close unnecessary programs

    This helps your computer run the video call smoothly

  • Charge your laptop (if applicable)

    Or make sure it's plugged in to avoid running out of battery

🏠 Setting Up Your Space

✅ DO:

  • Face a window for natural light on your face
  • Choose a quiet room
  • Position camera at eye level
  • Have a plain or tidy background
  • Sit in a comfortable chair
  • Keep water nearby

❌ DON'T:

  • Sit with a window behind you
  • Have a messy background
  • Point camera up at your face
  • Leave TV or radio on
  • Sit in a noisy area
  • Eat during the call

📞 During Your Call

  • Mute yourself when not speaking

    Reduces background noise for everyone else

  • Look at the camera when talking

    It's like making eye contact with the other person

  • Speak clearly and a bit slower

    Audio quality can sometimes make fast speech hard to understand

  • Use the chat for questions

    If you don't want to interrupt, type your question in the chat

🔧 Quick Fixes for Common Problems

Problem Try This
They can't hear me Check if you're muted (click the microphone icon)
I can't hear them Check your computer's volume; make sure speakers/headphones are connected
They can't see me Check if video is off (click the camera icon); check camera privacy settings
Video is choppy/freezing Turn off your video to save bandwidth; close other programs
I got disconnected Click the meeting link again to rejoin; check your internet
💡 When in Doubt: Leave the meeting and rejoin using the same link. This fixes most problems!
