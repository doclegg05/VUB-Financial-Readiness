💡 Quick Tips

  • Bookmark these sites! Save VA.gov and MyHealtheVet to your browser favorites.
  • One login works for all. Your VA.gov (ID.me) login works on all three portals.
  • Keep your info updated. Make sure your phone, email, and address are current in VA.gov.
  • Download the app! MyHealtheVet has a mobile app for your phone.

🛡️ Stay Safe Online

  • Always type the URL directly — don't click links in emails claiming to be from the VA
  • Look for .gov — Real VA sites end in .gov (not .com or .org)
  • Check for the lock icon — Make sure you see 🔒 in your browser's address bar
  • Never share your password — VA employees will NEVER ask for your password
